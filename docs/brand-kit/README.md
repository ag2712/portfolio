# Alisha Gonsalves: Personal Brand Kit

Source of truth for Alisha's personal brand. Use it for the portfolio site, resume, case-study decks, docs and anything published under her name. Live version: https://claude.ai/artifact/5qsGR5UvVqYrwc6P7yS9Fw

Calm to look at, cheeky to read. This is the personal brand of Alisha Gonsalves, Senior Product Designer. Use it for the portfolio, resume, case-study decks, docs and anything else published under her name. Two typefaces, a warm stone-and-ink palette, and plenty of white space.

## The brand in one line

**Calm page, lively voice.** The visuals stay quiet: warm stone paper, near-black Inter, and soft mist or sage only where the story turns. The words bring the personality.

## Voice and content

Alisha writes the way she talks. She is **sassy, bubbly and direct, with no fluff**. She was raised in the Middle East and has Indian heritage, so her phrasing carries a British polish that shows in how she speaks and types.

### Personality

- **Sassy.** Have a point of view and say it with a little wink. Cheek is welcome. Snark aimed at users or colleagues is not.
- **Bubbly.** Let real enthusiasm for the work and the people come through in word choice, not in exclamation marks. Use one "!" per page or slide at most.
- **Direct.** Say the point first. Cut warm-ups like "In this section I will talk about" or "It's important to note that".
- **No fluff.** If a word isn't doing a job, cut it. Keep sentences short, with one idea each.

### British mannerisms

- Use British spelling everywhere: *modernising*, *colour*, *learnt*, *organise*, *programme*, *centre*.
- British phrasing is welcome where it fits naturally: "rather", "quite", "brilliant", "sorted", "have a go", "a fair bit". Use a light touch, not a costume.
- Use "I" for her own work and "we" for team work.

### Punctuation and formatting

- **Never use em dashes (—).** Use a full stop, a comma, a colon or brackets instead.
  - Not: "Sole designer — end-to-end."
  - Yes: "Sole designer, end-to-end."
- For number ranges, write "to" ("2022 to 2024") or use a short en dash with no spaces ("2022–2024").
- Use sentence case for headings and buttons ("Selected work", not "Selected Work"). Write `label` lines in capitals.
- Don't use emoji in professional assets.

### Lead with the outcome

Back claims with real numbers: "$169.7K net-new ARR", "13.6% activation, 58% above goal", "760-vote #1 Service Hub request".

## Colour

The palette is warm stone and near-black, with three soft accent fills and one deep slate accent. It is quiet by design. Colour marks a moment and never decorates.

| Token | Light | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| `paper` | #F4F2EC | Every page and slide background |
| `sand` | #D8D5CB | Hairlines, table rules, card borders, tag fills |
| `rule` | #7C7C78 | Stronger rules, chart axes, outline icons |
| `ink` | #0A0A0A | Headings, display type and metrics |
| `ink-soft` | #1A1A1A | Body copy |
| `ink-muted` | #5A5A56 | Captions, dates and mono labels |
| `mist` | #DCE4E9 | Card and callout fills |
| `mist-deep` | #B7CAD3 | A full-bleed accent section or slide |
| `sage` | #D9E3CC | A second full-bleed accent, for outcomes |
| `slate` | #2C4150 | The accent: links, key numbers, primary buttons |

- Set nearly everything in `ink` and `ink-soft` on `paper`. Most pages and slides need no other colour.
- Use `mist` for cards and callouts, with `ink` text on top.
- Use `mist-deep` or `sage` as a full background only once or twice in a piece, to mark a turn in the story (for example "the insight" or "the outcome"). Never put them next to each other.
- Use `slate` for one thing per view: a key number, a link or the main button. Put `on-slate` text on slate fills.
- Use `ink-muted` only on `paper` and `mist`. Use `rule` for lines and icons, never for text.
- Never use gradients, pure black (#000) or bright, saturated colours.
- A dark theme exists for web use. On dark, paper turns charcoal (#141413), the fills deepen and slate lightens to #A9C0CC.

## Typography

Both typefaces are free Google Fonts, so they work the same in **Google Slides, Google Docs, Figma, Vercel and the web**.

- **Inter** (sans) is for all headings and body text. Use Semibold (600) for headings and Regular (400) for text.
- **IBM Plex Mono** (mono) is for small labels, captions and dates only. Never use it for paragraphs or headings.

### Web and Figma scale

| Style | Size / line height | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| `display` | 56 / 60 px | Inter 600, −2% tracking |
| `metric` | 48 / 52 px | Inter 600, in `ink` or `slate` |
| `heading-1` | 40 / 48 px | Inter 600 |
| `heading-2` | 28 / 36 px | Inter 600 |
| `heading-3` | 20 / 28 px | Inter 600 |
| `body-lg` | 18 / 28 px | Inter 400 |
| `body` | 16 / 26 px | Inter 400 |
| `small` | 14 / 22 px | Inter 400 |
| `label` | 12 / 16 px | Plex Mono 500, capitals, +8% tracking |
| `caption` | 13 / 20 px | Plex Mono 400 |

### Google Slides (16:9)

| Use | Font and size |
|---|---|
| Cover title | Inter Semibold 48–56 pt |
| Slide title | Inter Semibold 28–32 pt |
| Big number | Inter Semibold 40–48 pt, `ink` or `slate` |
| Body | Inter 14–18 pt, `ink` or `ink-soft` |
| Eyebrow and labels | IBM Plex Mono Medium 10–11 pt, capitals |
| Captions and sources | IBM Plex Mono 9–10 pt, `ink-muted` |

Set the slide background to `paper` (#F4F2EC) and keep a 0.5 inch margin on all sides.

### Google Docs and resume

| Use | Font and size |
|---|---|
| Name | Inter Semibold 20 pt, `ink` |
| Section headings | Inter Semibold 11–12 pt, `slate` |
| Role titles | Inter Semibold 11 pt |
| Body and bullets | Inter 10–10.5 pt, `ink-soft` |
| Dates and locations | IBM Plex Mono 9 pt, `ink-muted` |

To add the fonts in Google Slides or Docs, open the font menu, choose **More fonts**, then search for "Inter" and "IBM Plex Mono".

## Layout and spacing

- Build on a 4px base. Use `space-4` (16) for the tightest margins, `space-6` (24) for card padding, `space-16` (64) between major sections, and `space-24` (96) around a hero.
- Left-align text. Keep paragraphs about 65 characters wide.
- Place a `label` eyebrow above a heading, with `space-2` between them, then `space-8` before the content.
- Give each slide or section one idea and plenty of white space.

## Shape, borders and shadow

- Use `radius-md` (8px) for cards, buttons and case-study images, `radius-sm` (4px) for tags and inputs, and `radius-pill` for skill chips.
- Separate things with `sand` hairlines first. Use `shadow-card` only when a card truly needs to lift off the page.
- Never add coloured left-border accents to cards.

## Name mark and imagery

- **No logo yet.** Set the name as type: "Alisha Gonsalves" in Inter Semibold, `ink`, with "Senior Product Designer" beneath in `ink-soft`.
- For screenshots and product images, use `radius-md` and a `sand` border on `paper`, and caption them in `caption`.
- For diagrams and frameworks, use `ink` strokes, `slate` for the single path that matters, and `mist` or `sage` fills.
